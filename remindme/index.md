# Remind Me...

Never forget anything ever again! Your reminder is only one tap away.

Create simple alerts to remind me to...
- pick up laundry in 30 minutes
- do my dishes at 9pm

all in one tap.

Personalize your reminder app to fit your routine:
- Set default due dates, for important times of the day
- Add frequently used durations, like how long it takes to wash your laundry, or to run the dryer.

Personalize the frequency of alerts:
- Choose how many times to remind you for each task
- Customize how long before the app sends you a follow-up reminder
